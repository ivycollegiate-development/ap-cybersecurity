1. Warm-up challenge (15 min)
2. Main challenge arc (90 min) — student drives, Paul coaches
3. Writeup time (30 min) — GitHub repo under `ivycollegiate-development`,
   one writeup per session: what the challenge was, failed attempts included,

4. Next-session preview (5 min)
