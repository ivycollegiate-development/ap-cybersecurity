# U6-L01: Exam Debrief — What Surprised You, What You'd Answer Differently

**AP Cybersecurity — ICA 2026-27**  
**Date**: May 6, 2027 (Thu) | **Period**: 122 | **Duration**: 50 min  
**Type**: Post-AP Portfolio Launch | **Format**: Discussion + Reflection Writing

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:
1. **Analyze** their AP Exam experience against prepared content (metacognition)
2. **Identify** specific knowledge gaps and test-taking strategy adjustments
3. **Articulate** a concrete "if I could retake" action plan for future certifications
4. **Begin** portfolio assembly mindset: evidence of learning > score

---

## Materials

- AP Exam admission ticket (brought by students)
- Blank paper or digital doc for reflection
- `Portfolio-Template.md` (distributed, review Sections 1, 6)
- Timer for structured reflection

---

## Lesson Flow

| Time | Activity | Details |
|------|----------|---------|
| 0-5 | **Opening** | Welcome back! "The exam is done. The learning continues." Collect tickets (optional). Distribute Portfolio-Template. |
| 5-15 | **Structured Reflection** (10 min silent write) | Prompts on board — students write:<br>1. **Most surprising question/topic**: What caught you off guard?<br>2. **Best prepared area**: What clicked perfectly?<br>3. **Time management**: Did you finish? Flag & return work?<br>4. **FRQ approach**: Did you cite sources? Use key terms?<br>5. **One thing you'd change** about prep (not content). |
| 15-30 | **Pair-Share Debrief** (15 min) | Pair with someone you didn't study with. Share reflections (5 min each). Identify: 1 common surprise, 1 divergent experience. |
| 30-40 | **Whole-Class Synthesis** (10 min) | Teacher captures on board:<br>- **Surprises** → patterns (e.g., "Kubernetes", "CloudPolicy", "Multi-select wording")<br>- **Strategy wins** → "Flag & return saved me", "Cite source = points"<br>- **Strategy regrets** → "Spent 15 min on one MCQ", "Didn't outline FRQ" |
| 40-45 | **Portfolio Connection** | "This reflection = Portfolio Section 6 (Reflection & Growth). Your 'what I'd change' = your certification study plan." Show Portfolio-Template Section 6. |
| 45-50 | **Exit Ticket** | Write on card/index: **One specific action** you'll take this week toward a cybersecurity certification (e.g., "Watch Messer Security+ Domain 1 videos", "Create TryHackMe account"). Collect. |

---

## Teacher Notes

- **Tone**: Celebratory but analytical. No score discussion (scores come July).
- **No grading** on reflection — participation only.
- **Connect to U6-L04/L05**: Exit ticket actions feed directly into Career Path Research.
- **Portfolio-Template Section 6** prompts align with today's reflection.
