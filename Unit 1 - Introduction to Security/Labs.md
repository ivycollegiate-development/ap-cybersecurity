|-----|--------|------|------------------|
| Phishing email classification (Issues) | P2/P4 | [apcyber-u1-phishing-samples](https://github.com/ivycollegiate-development/apcyber-u1-phishing-samples) | fork → clone fork → open Issues per email pair |
