## Scoring Guide

**Part A — Adversary (2 pts)**
- 1 pt: Names a valid adversary type (state adversary, organized crime, insider threat)
- 1 pt: Cites scenario evidence (CEO impersonation, wire transfer, urgency, late Friday timing)

**Part B — Wireless attack (1 pt)**
- 1 pt: Names a valid wireless attack (evil twin, man-in-the-middle, rogue AP, deauth attack)
- *0 pts: Listing a non-wireless attack (phishing, malware)*

**Part C — Protections (3 pts)**
- Valid: VPN, HTTPS/certificate checking, no auto-connect, two-factor/MFA, company-managed device
- Each protection needs a brief explanation of *how* it helps (not just the name)
