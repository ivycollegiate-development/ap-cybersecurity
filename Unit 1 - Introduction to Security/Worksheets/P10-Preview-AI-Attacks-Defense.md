## Teacher Notes & Instructions

**Setup checklist:**
- Projector for the two-email comparison (have both emails printed as backup)
- Printed vocabulary sheet (or students copy from board)
- CED excerpt handouts for P12 pre-reading
- No student computers — paper day

**Pacing:** Quiz (10) → Primer (10) → Email comparison (15) → Vocabulary (10) → Exit ticket (5) = 50 min.

**Email comparison facilitation:**
- Use a real human-written phishing email (e.g., classic "your account is suspended" with grammar errors) vs an AI-generated version of the same theme.
- Take the class vote by show of hands BEFORE revealing. Expect the class to be split — that IS the point.
- Push students to name concrete tells: perfect grammar, personalized details (name, recent purchase), natural tone, no awkward phrasing, credible but fabricated urgency.
