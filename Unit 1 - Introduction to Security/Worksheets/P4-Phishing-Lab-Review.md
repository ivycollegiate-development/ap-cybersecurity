## Teacher Notes & Instructions

**Setup checklist:**
- Projector ready to display 3-4 select student Issues (anonymized if needed)
- Printed Issue summary sheet (optional, for students without their Issue loaded — paper day, no computers)
- Timer for peer review

**Pacing:** Quiz (10) → Class discussion (25) → Peer review (10) → Exit ticket (5) = 50 min.

**Discussion facilitation:**
- Call on 3-4 students with strong/different classifications first to seed the table.
- Push students to cite the exact email text that triggered their classification ("which sentence made you say Authority?").
- If the class converged on one tactic, ask "what would make an attacker choose a different tactic for a different victim?"

**Common misclassification to correct:** students often label Authority-based emails as Scarcity when the email also creates time pressure — both can apply; the dominant trigger wins.

**Peer review norms:** model one glow + one grow before pairing. Glow must be specific ("your reasoning about the g00gle.com domain was sharp"), not generic ("good job").

**Scoring:** Exit ticket is ungraded — immediate feedback only. The 10-min quiz is graded (Quizzes/Assessments 25%).

**Follow-up:** Homework due 20:30 — read Scenario 1B login log, underline 2 suspicious entries, one sentence on why each is suspicious. Feed the most-missed S.E.T. tactic into Friday's flex/review.
