# U6-L04: Credential Ladder — Certs, Degrees, and What Employers Really Ask

**AP Cybersecurity — ICA 2026-27**  
**Date**: May 11, 2027 (Tue) | **Period**: 125 | **Duration**: 50 min  
**Type**: Career Research | **Format**: Jigsaw + Personal Plan

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:
1. **Evaluate** certification ROI for target roles (cost, time, recognition, prerequisites)
2. **Map** a personal credential ladder: entry → associate → professional → specialty
3. **Distinguish** "checkbox certs" from "skill-proving certs" in employer eyes
4. **Draft** a 12-month certification study plan with milestones

## Lesson Flow

| Time | Activity | Details |
|------|----------|---------|
| 0-5 | **Hook** | Show: "CISSP requires 5 years exp. Security+ requires 0. Which gets you hired *now*?" Discuss: certs ≠ skills, but certs open doors. |
| 5-20 | **Jigsaw: Cert Deep-Dive** (15 min) | 5 groups, each becomes expert on one cert track:<br>**Group A**: Security+ → CySA+ (Blue Team/SOC)<br>**Group B**: Security+ → PenTest+ (Offensive)<br>**Group C**: Security+ → SSCP → CISSP (Management/Generalist)<br>**Group D**: OSCP path (OffSec) — no Security+ prereq<br>**Group E**: Cloud (AWS Security Spec / AZ-500 / GCP)<br>Each group fills matrix row: Cost, Time, Prereqs, Roles unlocked, Employer perception, Study resources |
| 20-30 | **Teach-Back & Synthesis** (10 min) | Each group 2 min: "If you want [role], start with [cert] because..." Teacher captures on board: **Decision tree** → Role → First Cert → Second Cert |
| 30-40 | **Employer Reality Check** (10 min) | Read 5 anonymized hiring manager quotes:<br>1. "OSCP > degree for pen test roles"<br>2. "Security+ gets you past HR filter for SOC"<br>3. "CISSP without experience = paper tiger"<br>4. "Cloud certs matter more than CISSP for cloud roles"<br>5. "GitHub > certs for AppSec"<br>Discuss: **What's the pattern?** (Role-specific > generic; hands-on > knowledge-only) |
| 40-48 | **Personal 12-Month Plan** (8 min) | Students draft in Career-Path-Research.md Part 6:<br>- **Month 1-3**: Target cert ______ — Resources: ______ — Milestone: ______<br>- **Month 4-6**: Target cert ______ — Resources: ______ — Milestone: ______<br>- **Month 7-12**: Target cert ______ — Resources: ______ — Milestone: ______<br>- **Budget**: $______ (exam + materials)<br>- **Accountability**: Study group / mentor / schedule |
| 48-50 | **Commit & Share** | Volunteers share first-cert target. Teacher collects plans (photo/scan) for accountability. |

## Teacher Notes

- **Taiwan context**: Emphasize Security+ (DoD 8570 baseline for gov), CISSP (senior roles), Cloud certs (AWS/Azure Taiwan demand)
- **Cost awareness**: Security+ ~$400, OSCP ~$1600 — factor into student budgets
- **Timeline**: Security+ study = 8-12 weeks; OSCP = 3-6 months; CISSP = 4-6 months + experience
- **No "best" cert** — only "best for your target role"

## Differentiation

| Need | Adjustment |
|------|------------|
