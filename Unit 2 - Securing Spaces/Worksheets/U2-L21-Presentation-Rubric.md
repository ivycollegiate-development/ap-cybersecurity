## Teacher Notes

**Common gaps to watch for:**
- 2.3.B environmental controls (humidity/temp sensors, fire suppression) for gear closet — often missed
- 2.4.A log sources beyond badge logs (doorbell events, router logs, sensor alerts, guard patrol, visitor log)
- 2.4.C named response owners + specific actions (not "call the landlord")
- 2.2.C typhoon/flood resilience (Taiwan context) — elevated gear shelf, flood sensors, dual power feed, backup power for readers

**Peer-check format:** Trio rotation (A→B, B→C, C→A). Each reviewer writes findings on sticky notes placed on the sheet. Authors have 3 minutes to add/correct. Teacher spot-checks sticky notes for quality.

**Gap hunt:** After peer-check, class reads LO codes off sheets. Teacher writes on board. **Which learning objective did no student cover?** That gap → re-teach board for Nov 9.
