### 2. Risk Assessment (2.1.E)
A school's main server room has no fire suppression system. The likelihood of a fire is rated **Medium**, and the impact if a fire occurs is **Critical**. Using the standard likelihood × impact risk matrix, what is the **inherent risk rating**?

A. Low  
B. Medium  
C. High  
D. Critical

### 5. Detecting Physical Attacks (2.4.A–2.4.C)

The CCTV for that corridor has a 4-minute gap at 02:12–02:16. Which detection method **most directly** reveals this anomaly?

A. Badge log correlation with shift schedules  
B. Guard patrol variance report  
C. CCTV gap analysis  
D. Visitor log cross-reference

## Answer Key (Teacher Use Only)

| Q | Correct | CED Objective | Rationale |
|---|---------|---------------|-----------|
| 1 | **A** | 2.1.A | Reconnaissance is the first phase where adversaries gather information about the target (network topology, open ports, services, employee info). Initial Access (B) is gaining a foothold; Lateral Movement (C) is moving inside the network; Exfiltration (D) is stealing data. |
| 2 | **C** | 2.1.E | Medium likelihood × Critical impact = **High** inherent risk. Standard matrix: Medium × Critical falls in High. Low would require Low likelihood; Critical would require High likelihood. Residual risk would be lower after mitigation (e.g., adding fire suppression). |
| 3 | **C** | 2.2.B | A door held open by a doorstop (C) defeats the lock entirely, allowing anyone to tailgate. (A) logs entries but doesn't prevent tailgating if someone follows an authorized user. (B) is a strong control (mantrap). (D) is a procedural control, not a physical vulnerability. |
| 4 | **C** | 2.3.B | Environmental controls (HVAC, fire suppression, humidity) protect the **room layer** where equipment resides. Perimeter (A) = fences, lighting, bollards. Building (B) = locks, alarms, guards. Rack (D) = cable locks, port locks. |
| 5 | **A** | 2.4.B | Badge log correlation with shift schedules (A) directly flags an off-hours access by an employee who should not be there. Guard patrol variance (B) and CCTV gap (C) are supporting evidence but don't identify *who* accessed. Visitor log (D) is irrelevant — this is an employee badge. |
