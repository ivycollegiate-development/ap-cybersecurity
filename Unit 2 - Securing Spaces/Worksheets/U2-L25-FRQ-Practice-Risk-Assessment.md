## Scenario

A 40-unit apartment building in Taichung has one entry door with a keypad, a CCTV camera at the parking entrance, and a badge log for staff. Residents report that unknown people have entered several times in the past month. The building's switch closet is on the ground floor.

---

## Prompt

Write a **risk assessment** for the building and recommend **treatment for two** of the findings you identify.

---

## Before you write — the three requirements

Write these three lines at the top of your answer page. Every sentence after that should serve one of them.

1. This is a **risk assessment** — likelihood and impact, not a description of the building.
2. Exactly **two treatments**, not five.
3. You are **not** redesigning the building.

---

## What earns the points

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Identifies at least **two physical vulnerabilities** correctly, each labelled with its CED category (2.2.A, 2.2.B, 2.2.C) | 2 |
| Gives a **likelihood** and an **impact** for each, each with a one-sentence reason | 2 |
| Selects a **correct treatment** (avoid / transfer / mitigate / accept) for each, with justification | 4 |
| Addresses the **ground-floor switch closet** and a **typhoon/flood or shared-building-network** condition | 2 |
| **Total** | **10** |

The rubric is worth as much as the answer. The two points for the Taiwan conditions are the most commonly lost.
