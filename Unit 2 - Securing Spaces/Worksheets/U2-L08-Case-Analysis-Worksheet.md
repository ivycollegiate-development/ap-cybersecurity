## 1: Read your brief

Read the case brief assigned to your group. Do not summarize the story. You are answering three questions about it, and those three questions are the format of a scored AP Cybersecurity FRQ.

## 2: The three questions

| FRQ question | Your answer |
| ------------ | ----------- |
| Attack method — name the physical vector specifically, not "social engineering" | |
| Vulnerability exploited — the weakness in the system, not in the person | |
| Control that would have stopped it — specific, and not dependent on perfect human behavior | |

## 3: Be exact

Vagueness costs points. "Unverified contractor admitted by badge escort at a side door" is an answer. "Someone tricked their way in" is not.

Your control must not depend on the attacker being less clever or the insider being better. If your best control does depend on human behavior, say so and propose a second one that does not.

## 4: Detection

Was there a detection control in place at all for this attack? Circle one.

Yes, and it would have caught it · Yes, but it would not have caught it · No, there was none

What signal would have caught it? Name the pattern, not just the event — unusual volume, unusual hours, unusual destinations, an entry that should not have been possible.
