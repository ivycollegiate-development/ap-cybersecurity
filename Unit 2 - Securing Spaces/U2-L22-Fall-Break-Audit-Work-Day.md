## Activities

1. **Clarification & Questions (15 min):** Open floor for questions about the audit checklist, what counts as acceptable evidence, and the two Taiwan addenda — the addenda are the part students most often get wrong. Review the sample audit margin notes: why 2.2.A vs 2.2.C, why likelihood medium not high, why avoid vs mitigate.

2. **Independent Work Time (55 min):** Students work on their audits. Teacher circulates for 1-on-1 check-ins:
   - Verify each finding has: description, likelihood, impact, treatment, evidence (4 lines minimum)
   - Check Taiwan Addendum A: shared network risk + evidence (not opinion)
   - Check Taiwan Addendum B: typhoon/flood gap + what it breaks
   - Confirm risk line uses likelihood × impact language
   - Confirm treatment line names avoid/transfer/mitigate/accept + reasoning sentence

3. **Study Guide Progress (10 min):** Students continue Unit 2 study guide Section 1 (every objective code + first control for home site). Teacher spot-checks for completeness.

4. **Final Reminders (5 min):**

   - **Length:** 2–4 pages, hand-written. One page minimum per addendum.
   - **Bring:** Audit checklist + two-sentence answer to each Taiwan addendum.
   - **Grading:** Reasoning, not the building.
   - Nov 9–12: four review days driven by class audit findings. Test Friday, Nov 13.
