## Activities

1. **Attendance and check-in (3 min):** Confirm who still owes a Unit 2 lab submission. Write your name against the item on the printed tracker. If you have no idea what you owe, say so — that is a conversation, not a penalty.

2. **Silent catch-up work (25 min):** Complete any outstanding Unit 2 lab submission from the tracker, or use the time to start or finish the Unit 2 study guide. Work independently; raise your hand if you are stuck on a specific item and I will come to you.

3. **Unit 3 preview — quiet reading (14 min):** Read the first CED excerpt of Unit 3 (Securing Networks). Read it once through without taking notes. It is here so the first day back is not zero, not so you can be tested on it. Three things worth noticing as you read: which vocabulary words are brand new, and which are old words from Unit 2 wearing new clothes.

4. **Close-out (3 min):** Anything still open goes on the tracker and gets first claim on Monday. Have the audit packet ready to hand in Monday morning.

**Fall Break starts now** and runs through Sunday, Nov 8. Unit 2 continues the following week, and the **Unit 2 Test is Friday, Nov 13.** Do the physical audit — notebook, no computers, no GitHub. Do not open a laptop for this course during the break.

**Homework (due Mon Nov 9 — graded HW quiz that day):** Complete the Fall Break physical security audit, 2–4 pages, hand-written, including both Taiwan addenda. Read the Unit 3 preview excerpt.

**Differentiation / ELL support:** The tracker is a checklist, so students can see exactly what is outstanding without having to track it themselves. The Unit 3 preview is a short read with no questions attached — students who want structure can list the section headings only. The first period back is a graded quiz, so tell students now, not Monday morning.
