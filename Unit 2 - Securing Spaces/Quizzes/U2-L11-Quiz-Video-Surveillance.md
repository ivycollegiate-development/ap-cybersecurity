# U2 L11 — Homework Quiz: Video Surveillance & Access Logs

**Date:** Oct 14, 2026 (Wed)  
**Time:** 5 min  
**Format:** 3 MCQ — paper (paper day quiz convention)

---

1. **A security camera covering a server room door is an example of which type of control?**
   a) Preventive  
   b) Detective  
   c) Corrective  
   d) Compensating  

2. **A badge reader that logs every credential presentation (timestamp, badge ID, door, direction, grant/deny) is primarily a ________ control.**
   a) Preventive  
   b) Detective  
   c) Corrective  
   d) Deterrent  

3. **What critical information does a badge access log record that a security camera does NOT?**
   a) The exact time a person entered the space  
   b) The identity of the credential presented (badge ID)  
   c) A visual record of the person's face and clothing  
   d) Whether the door was held open after a valid badge swipe  

---

**Answer Key:**  
1. b — Cameras *detect* (record) events; they do not physically prevent entry.  
2. b — Badge logs *detect* and record credential use; the reader itself is preventive (grants/denies), but the log is a detective record.  
3. b — The log ties an event to a specific badge ID (credential identity). A camera shows a person but cannot reliably identify *who* the badge belongs to.
