## Part B — Answer Key

**Answer sequence:** 1-A, 2-A, 3-C

### 1. A — 2.1.E (inherent vs residual)

Inherent risk is the rating **before** controls: Low/High. Residual is the rating **after**: Low/Medium. The control did not move likelihood — it was Low both times — it moved **impact**, from High to Medium.

- **B** swaps the two ratings: it makes the inherent the *after* state.
- **C** treats the residual as unchanged, which contradicts the re-rating.
- **D** confuses transfer with mitigation; no third party is involved.

The full-credit habit: state the **inherent pair**, state the **residual pair**, then name **which half the control moved**. Students who can do that can answer any version of this item.

### 2. A — 2.1.F (risk treatment)

**Avoid** means eliminating the activity that creates the risk. Storing the scans *is* the activity, and management stops doing it and destroys what exists.

- **B (transfer)** moves the financial consequence to a third party — no insurer is involved here.
- **C (mitigate)** reduces likelihood or impact but keeps the activity.
- **D (accept)** documents the residual and takes no action — the opposite of what happened.

The distinction that separates C from A: **mitigation manages the risk; avoidance removes the source of it.**

### 3. C — 2.1.E (ALE)

SLE = NT$6,000,000. ARO = 1 in 4 years = 0.25. **ALE = SLE × ARO = NT$6,000,000 × 0.25 = NT$1,500,000.**

- **A** is the SLE, not the annualized figure.
- **B** multiplies by 4 instead of dividing — ARO is events per year, so a once-in-four-years event has ARO 0.25, not 4.
- **D** halves the ALE (a 1-in-8-year rate).

The mark of a correct answer is **both** the number and the arithmetic. The quiz provides a "Show your arithmetic (SLE × ARO)" line under Q3, so a right number with the arithmetic not shown earns 1 of 2.
