# U2 L16 Quiz — Physical Security Policy & Environmental Controls (CED 2.3)

**3 MCQ — 5 minutes — Paper day quiz format**

---

**1.** A facility's server room has a badge reader on the door, a camera covering the entry, and a temperature sensor. The badge reader logs every entry. The camera records continuously. The temperature sensor triggers an alert at 28°C. Which of these is a **detective control**?

A. The badge reader  
B. The camera  
C. The temperature sensor  
D. The badge log review conducted each morning by the facilities lead

**Answer: D** — A detective control discovers an event after it occurs. The badge reader, camera, and temperature sensor are all *preventive* or *monitoring* devices; the **review of the badge log** is the detective control because it identifies anomalies after the fact. (CED 2.3.A, 2.3.C)

---

**2.** An organization's physical security policy states: "All visitors must be escorted at all times." During an audit, you find that the reception desk has no visitor log, no badge printer, and no escort procedure documented. Which learning objective does this gap most directly violate?

A. 2.3.A — Locks and access controls  
B. 2.3.B — Surveillance / CCTV  
C. 2.3.C — Environmental and monitoring controls  
D. 2.3.A and 2.3.C — Access control scoping and policy auditability

**Answer: D** — The missing visitor log and escort procedure are **access control scoping** (2.3.A: who can enter, under what conditions) and **policy auditability** (2.3.C: policies must have owners, review cycles, and produce records an auditor accepts). Cameras (2.3.B) are not the primary gap here.

---

**3.** A data center in Taichung sits on the ground floor. Typhoon season brings wind-driven rain and flooding risk. The facility has: raised flooring, a sump pump, UPS for badge readers and network gear, and a single roof drain. Which environmental control is **most likely insufficient** for typhoon resilience?

A. Raised flooring  
B. Sump pump  
C. UPS for badge readers and network gear  
D. Single roof drain path

**Answer: D** — A **single roof drain path** is a single point of failure during extreme rainfall. If it clogs, water backs up and overwhelms the sump pump. Redundant drainage is a standard typhoon/flood resilience requirement (CED 2.3.C). The other three are appropriate controls.

---

**Answer Key:** 1-D, 2-D, 3-D
