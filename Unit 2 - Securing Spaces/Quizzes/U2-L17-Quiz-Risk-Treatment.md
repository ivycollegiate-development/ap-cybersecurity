# U2 L17 Quiz — Risk Treatment Options (2.1.F)

**3 MCQ — 5 minutes — Tech day quiz format**

---

**1.** A risk assessment identifies that a ground-floor server room in Taichung has a **high likelihood** of flooding during typhoon season and **high impact** (equipment loss, data unavailability). The organization decides to **relocate the server room to the second floor**. Which risk treatment option does this represent?

A. Avoid  
B. Transfer  
C. Mitigate  
D. Accept

**Answer: A** — **Avoid** eliminates the risk by removing the asset from the threat environment. Moving the server room upstairs removes the flood exposure entirely. (CED 2.1.F)

---

**2.** The same organization cannot relocate the server room due to structural constraints. Instead, they purchase a **flood insurance policy** that covers equipment replacement and business interruption losses. They also install water sensors and a backup generator. The insurance policy is an example of which risk treatment?

A. Avoid  
B. Transfer  
C. Mitigate  
D. Accept

**Answer: B** — **Transfer** shifts the financial impact to a third party (the insurer). The water sensors and generator are *mitigation* (reducing likelihood/impact), but the insurance itself is *transfer*. (CED 2.1.F)

---

**3.** After relocating the server room (avoid) and buying insurance (transfer), a residual risk remains: a **tailgating attack** at the second-floor server room door. The organization installs a **mantrap with anti-tailgating sensors** and assigns a guard to monitor the entry during business hours. This is an example of:

A. Avoid — the mantrap eliminates tailgating  
B. Transfer — the guard is a third-party contractor  
C. Mitigate — the controls reduce likelihood and impact of tailgating  
D. Accept — tailgating cannot be fully prevented

**Answer: C** — **Mitigate** reduces either the likelihood or the impact (or both) of a risk that cannot be avoided or fully transferred. The mantrap and guard reduce the *likelihood* of successful tailgating; they do not eliminate the risk (avoid) or shift it (transfer). (CED 2.1.F)

---

**Answer Key:** 1-A, 2-B, 3-C
