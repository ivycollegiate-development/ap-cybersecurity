# U2 L27: Unit 2 Review Day 4 (Nov 12, Thu — PAPER DAY)

**⚡ HW Quiz (8 min):** Graded HW quiz — the two questions you missed on the Nov 11 sprint, re-asked cold, plus 3 new MCQ on 2.1 risk assessment and treatment.

**Learning Objectives:**
- 2.1.E Distinguish inherent risk from residual risk and state how a control moves a rating
- 2.3.A Justify a physical control choice with reference to how the attack actually works
- 2.4.C Design an alerting threshold and describe the response it triggers

**Materials:** Graded HW quiz (printed), Nov 11 sprint results and item analysis, study guide, Friday's test logistics sheet

**No computers today.** Paper day. **This is the last period before the test.**

---

## Activities

1. **Graded HW quiz (8 min):** The two missed sprint questions return first — that pattern recurs in the test, and the point of re-asking is to see whether the gap closed. Then 3 new MCQ on risk assessment and treatment. Collected.

2. **Targeted weak-area review (14 min):** Driven entirely by the Nov 11 sprint results. Take the two weakest objectives from the sprint — not from today's quiz, from the sprint, because the sprint is the diagnostic that matters.

   For each: state the misconception, work one fresh example, run a check question. If the two weakest objectives overlap with what was re-taught on Monday or Tuesday, do not repeat that re-teach — go a level deeper instead, into the application form the test will ask for. Depth, not repetition, is what is left.

3. **Open Q&A — last chance (13 min):** This is the last period before the test. Take every question, including the ones that feel small. Format: I answer three questions cold from the front, then a written round for anything else on an anonymous slip so nobody has to self-select to ask.

   If nobody has a question, ask one: *"You are explaining defense in depth to a Taichung building manager who has budget for exactly one control. Which one, and why that one?"* Let two or three students answer. It is a 2.1 and 2.3 question and it is worth thinking about before Friday.

4. **Study guide finalization (6 min):** Hands on your guide. By the end of today it is complete: every CED objective in 2.1 through 2.4, one sentence in your own words, one real example each. If your guide has a section written in the teacher's phrasing, rewrite that section today — you will not have your book Friday.

5. **Test logistics (4 min):**
   - **Friday, Nov 13 — Unit 2 Test.** Paper. No computers.
   - **20 MCQ plus 1 FRQ, 45 minutes.** Budget about 1 minute 40 seconds per MCQ and reserve the last 12–13 minutes for the FRQ — do not spend two full minutes on every MCQ and arrive with no time to write.
   - **MCQ coverage:** 2.1 attack phases, 2.1 risk assessment, 2.1 risk treatment, 2.2 physical vulnerabilities, 2.3 physical protections, 2.4 detection methods. Study guide sections map onto it directly.
   - **You will be given** one blank scratch sheet and a pencil. No notes, no study guide, no phone, no watch alarms.
   - **The FRQ is one question, not five.** You will be told exactly what it asks for and given the rubric. A common way to lose points is to answer a question that was not asked.

**Homework (due Fri — test day):** No homework. Sleep. Have the study guide in your bag, complete, and do not open it after 21:00.

**Differentiation / ELL support:** The review is driven by the sprint item analysis, so the objectives covered are announced at the start of the block and students know where to aim. The anonymous slip format for questions means a student who will not ask aloud is still served. The test logistics are on a printed sheet students keep, so timing and coverage do not depend on having heard the announcement. Provide the CED objective list printed alongside the study guide so students can check their own coverage section by section.
